# Helix Bioworks: Enzyme Screening Programme, Q3 Review

Prepared by the Helix Bioworks screening team for the programme steering group.
All figures in this document are fictional and exist only for tool development.

## Summary

The screening platform has now run for three quarters. Hit rate per screened library
rose from 4% in Q1 to 7% in Q2 and 9% in Q3. The team recommends funding a
production-temperature validation run in Q4.

## How the screen works

Each candidate enzyme passes through four stages: library design, expression in the
host strain, the activity assay, and ranking. Variants that fail expression never reach
the assay, so expression yield limits how many variants can be tested.

The activity gain comes from a stabilising mutation near the active site. The mutation
rigidifies the binding loop, which raises substrate affinity, which raises turnover at
the assay temperature. At higher temperatures the same rigidity is expected to reduce
unfolding, but this feedback has not been measured.

## Results

| Quarter | Libraries screened | Variants tested | Hits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 10 | 1200 | 48 |
| Q2 | 12 | 1500 | 105 |
| Q3 | 12 | 1550 | 140 |

The best Q3 variant, HX-212, showed 3.1 times the activity of the starting enzyme.

## Risks

Activity has only been measured at 30 C. Production runs at 45 C.
Only one host strain has been used, so expression results may not transfer.
